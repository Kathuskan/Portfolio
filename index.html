IBM Stock Forecasting & Uncertainty Analysis

16,272 Observations MAE: 4.4037 | RMSE: 7.2174 83.33% PI Coverage

Time-series forecasting study on 16,272 Yahoo Finance IBM stock records using Azure Databricks and PySpark for data preparation, and Python/Statsmodels for stationarity & autocorrelation modeling.

  • Compared ARIMA(1,1,0), ARIMA(0,1,1) & ARIMA(1,1,1) vs. previous-close baseline.
  • Chronological splits & sequential 1-day-ahead validation on 414 held-out days.
  • Evaluated 90% historical-quantile intervals (83.33% empirical coverage).
